Requires a result column: r or pnl_r (R multiple), or pnl / pnl_usd. Add symbol and date to score G4, and side (long/short) to score G5. Rows must be in time order.
The more variants you test, the more likely one looks good by chance. The Deflated Sharpe Ratio adjusts for this.

Results will appear here.

What the six gates check
G1 Sufficient sample. Trades ≥ the larger of 50 and MinTRL, the number of trades needed to be 95% sure the edge is real. Thinner or noisier edges need more trades.
G2 Not just luck. Probabilistic Sharpe PSR(0) ≥ 0.95 and Deflated Sharpe (after the number of variants you tried) ≥ 0.5.
G3 Still working recently. Average of the last 100 trades > 0 and of the last 30 trades > −0.1 per trade.
G4 Not reliant on a few coins or days. Top 3 coins < 50% of gross profit and best 2 days < 50% of net profit.
G5 No losing side. Fails if the long side or the short side alone has 50+ trades with a negative average.
G6 Acceptable drawdown. Maximum peak-to-trough drawdown below 15R (scored only for R-multiple input).

This tool cannot detect look-ahead bias in code, or model real fees, slippage and funding. It only scores the trades you give it.
